Kimodo achieves 55\% success versus ARDY's 40\%, an observed 15 percentage-point advantage (Table~\ref{tab:generators}). There are 23 shared successes, 28 shared failures, 32 Kimodo-only successes, and 17 ARDY-only successes. ARDY produces the first reference faster and attains slightly lower joint-tracking RMSE, while Kimodo converts more executions into retained grasps.

\paragraph{Initial lifting versus sustained manipulation}
The clearest distinction appears after the block first satisfies the task threshold. \emph{Each generator reaches the clearance-and-contact threshold in 58/100 trials}, but ARDY retains 40 of these grasps through final assessment, compared with 55 for Kimodo. The sticky loss flag records 18 later losses for ARDY and three for Kimodo: 31.0\% versus 5.2\% of threshold-reaching trials. Sixteen ARDY losses receive the terminal reason \texttt{grasp\_lost\_after\_success}; the other two terminate with prohibited contact. This distinguishes a convincing initial lift from sustained object control and explains why similar tracking accuracy can yield different task success.

\paragraph{Motion locality and grasp geometry}
In the selected videos, ARDY sometimes couples the right-hand action with broader body motion, while Kimodo's grasp adjustments appear more localized. Whole-body coupling can change wrist--object geometry during closing and holding. Both methods use the same finger-controller settings and SONIC stabilization; their generated wrist geometry and subsequent motion differ. This makes contact-compatible reference motion a useful complement to joint-tracking accuracy.

Kimodo's dominant remaining bottleneck is alignment: 28 trials stop before acquisition, versus four acquisition failures, nine timeouts, and three post-threshold losses. ARDY has 17 acquisition failures and 18 timeouts in addition to its retention losses. The paired examples illustrate complementary strengths: Kimodo lifts while ARDY times out at seed 60000, but ARDY succeeds where Kimodo misses alignment at seed 60004. These results motivate adding execution-aware correction to the faster ARDY nominal chain, targeting contact and retention without replacing the generator.
